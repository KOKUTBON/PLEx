# Support PLEx and KOKUTBON

Open technology for the benefit of humankind — built by people, for people, and freely reusable by everyone.

## GitHub Sponsors

KOKUTBON is the designated recipient for this project: [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/KOKUTBON).
The integration is configured in [.github/FUNDING.yml](.github/FUNDING.yml). On 9 October 2026, the owner submitted KOKUTBON’s GitHub Sponsors application. The organization profile and $5, $25, $100 and $500 monthly and one-time tiers are configured. Payments await GitHub’s approval of the listing. Until then, contribute code, documentation, translations, testing, accessibility work or issue triage; no payment is required to participate.

## What support funds

Maintenance, security fixes, accessible interfaces, documentation, testing, reproducible examples and shared infrastructure. Support sustains the complete open-source project; it does not buy exclusive features, ownership, preferential review, governance votes or access to private user data. Existing open-source license rights apply equally to everyone, including commercial users.

## Accountability

@reiidoda coordinates technical stewardship. KOKUTBON organization owners administer the Sponsors account. Before spending sponsorship receipts, record the approved purpose and budget; publish aggregate receipts, fees, expenses, remaining balance and delivered work at least quarterly after payments activate. Do not publish donors' private identity, financial details, or contact information without consent. Funding with incompatible exclusivity or closed-result conditions is declined.

No claim of registered charitable status or tax deductibility is made. Payment processing, fees, cancellations and refunds follow GitHub Sponsors' terms and the recipient's applicable obligations. No cryptocurrency addresses or alternative payment accounts are authorized by this file.
