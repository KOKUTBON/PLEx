# Third Party Notices

## Source and redistribution policy

The project code is distributed under **Apache-2.0** in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Preserve existing author copyright statements and any NOTICE files. Dependencies retain their upstream licenses; project licensing does not relicense dependencies, models, fonts, maps, images or datasets.

Use the committed manifests, lockfiles and upstream license files as the dependency inventory. Before redistributing a binary/container or adding external data/model assets, record its exact version/source, license, required attribution and redistribution permissions. Generated personal records and production exports are not release assets. Models or external datasets downloaded at runtime remain subject to their own terms and are not granted a new license by this repository.

No provenance or license review can establish ownership of material whose origin is undocumented. Escalate unclear rights to KOKUTBON owners before publication; use synthetic fixtures where rights or privacy are uncertain.

## Initial source dependency inventory

[THIRD_PARTY_INVENTORY.json](THIRD_PARTY_INVENTORY.json) records available version/license metadata from lockfiles and local validation. It may include optional and development packages; it is not legal clearance or a binary/container SBOM. Preserve upstream license files when redistributing dependencies. Unrecorded terms must be checked upstream before bundling the affected component.
